Tactile data for robot hands

Teaching robots to feel.

Robots learned to see — but not to feel. Sezgi is a glove that records how people touch things, so robot hands can learn it.

See how it works Talk to our team
Built by a team of robotics world champions · 5+ years each in robotics engineering Grounded in research from MIT CSAIL · Meta FAIR · TACROSS
01 · The gap

Robots can see.
They can’t feel.

Robot hands fail at the grasp. Once the fingers close around an object, the camera has nothing left to measure — and pressure was never in the frame to begin with.

02 · How it works
1

People work in the glove

Sewing, cooking, assembling — normal work, in a thin fabric glove.

2

It records every touch

500 sensors across the whole hand capture where and how hard each finger presses.

3

Robots learn to feel

That data trains robot hands to grip like a human — without crushing or dropping.
